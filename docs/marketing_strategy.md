# Marketing Strategy - MakeYour.vote

### Primary Segments
1. **Policy Influencers**
   - Think tanks
   - Research organizations
   - Government agencies
   - Policy makers

2. **Enterprise Clients**
   - Market research firms
   - Political consulting agencies
   - Media organizations
   - Corporate strategy teams

3. **Active Citizens**
   - Age: 25-45
   - Education: College+
   - Interest in civic engagement

### Secondary Segments
- Academic institutions
- NGOs
- Political organizations
- Social activists
